Industry Challenges

Businesses need to improve agent efficiency, balance outreach cost and performance, and manage multiple communication channels across global markets.

Agent Capacity Needs to Be Fully Unlocked

Agents should spend their time talking to real customers. However, high voicemail rates and inconsistent number validity result in significant time wasted on ineffective calls.

Balancing Outreach Cost and Effectiveness

Voice delivers strong conversion but costs more than text channels. Businesses need to segment customers by value and choose the most cost-effective channel mix for outreach at scale.

Complexity of Global Channel Management

WhatsApp, SMS, Voice, and other channels coexist across markets. Managing multiple separate systems makes it difficult to unify customer data and deliver a consistent service experience.

Industry Solutions

Optimize every customer touchpoint with intelligent outbound calling, AI-powered engagement, and unified omnichannel communications built to scale globally.

Predictive Outbound Calling

Keep Agents Focused on Meaningful Conversations

For high-volume outbound scenarios such as telesales and payment reminders, NXLINK predicts agent availability and call answer probability, filters unsuccessful calls, and connects agents only with answered calls—improving utilization and productivity while keeping call abandonment rates low.

Multimodal AI Agent Outreach

Optimize Cost and Conversion Across Every Interaction

NXLINK combines voice broadcasts, IVR, natural voice interactions, and WhatsApp/SMS follow-ups to match customers with the right engagement method—reducing overall outreach costs while maintaining conversion performance.

Unified Omnichannel Management

Scale Customer Engagement Globally

NXLINK connects major communication and social channels in one platform, centralizing conversations, data, and agent workflows to deliver consistent customer experiences across markets and scale operations efficiently.
