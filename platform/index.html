Platform Overview

NXLINK is a cloud-based intelligent contact center that integrates omni-channel capabilities such as voice, SMS, and social media, resolving the fragmentation of traditional call center platforms. Leveraging AI, it enhances customer service scenarios including intelligent marketing, intelligent Q&A, and intelligent outbound calling, thereby improving service quality, boosting efficiency, and elevating the customer experience.

From Intake to Service, From Campaigns to Insights

NXLINK powers your complete customer engagement loop with AI.

Intelligent Omnichannel Intake

  • Unified Channel AccessAll-in-one support for WhatsApp, LINE, Viber, Zalo, SMS, Email, Messenger & Voice.
